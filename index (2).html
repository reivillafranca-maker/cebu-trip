01 Clark to Moalboal Moalboal Thu, Nov 19
11:45 AM
Depart Clark International Airport
By plane.
Clark → Mactan-Cebu: ~460 km, ~1 hr 30 min by air
1:15 PM
Land at Mactan-Cebu International Airport (MCIA)
Allow about 30 min to clear baggage claim.
1:45 PM
Lunch at the airport — Filipino Delights (Terminal 1, Domestic South Wing)
Open 24 hours, reimagined Filipino classics with locally sourced ingredients — right in the domestic terminal where you'll land. Jollibee nearby if you'd rather keep it quick and familiar. Eat before your van pickup — food options thin out fast once you're on the road south.
~2:45 PM
Van pickup, transfer to Moalboal
Typically 2.5–3 hrs, but figure closer to 4 hrs door-to-door with traffic.
Mactan Airport → Moalboal: ~95 km by van
~6:45 PM
Arrive & check in at Kuadros Hotel & Suites
Settle in, unpack, shower off the travel day.
7:30 PM
Dinner — Chili Bar or Native's Chicken
Chili Bar for grilled seafood and a laid-back beachfront setting; Native's Chicken if you'd rather keep it quick, cheap, and casual. Both are on the Panagsama strip.
Hotel → either spot: ~5–10 min walk
Night
Call it a dayFree time
Early lights-out — tomorrow starts with canyoneering and you'll want the energy.
02 Kawasan Falls canyoneering + night massage Moalboal Fri, Nov 20
6:00 AM
Breakfast
Included at Kuadros Hotel & Suites — eat well, it's a physical day.
6:45 AM
Habal-habal/van to Badian
Going early beats the tour-bus crowds and the midday heat.
Moalboal → Kawasan Falls: ~21–22 km, 30–45 min by habal-habal Habal-habal ~₱300–400 round trip, shared
7:30 AM
Register & gear up
Private canyoneering package for the 4 of you — own pace, guide, helmet, and life vest included. Worth confirming with the operator whether the quoted rate already covers all 4 pax or is per person.
₱2,000 private tour + ₱600 zipline add-on
8:00 AM – 1:00 PM
Canyoneering to Kawasan Falls, plus zipline
Cliff jumps, rope swings, and river trekking down to the turquoise falls, with the zipline add-on over the falls before or after the trek.
1:30 PM
Lunch — Kawasan Falls Cabin Restaurant
Right at the falls entrance — grilled fish, rice meals, cold drinks. The main basin is free to swim in a bit longer while you dry off.
2:30 PM
Head back to Moalboal
Same habal-habal/van route in reverse.
Kawasan Falls → Moalboal: ~21–22 km, 30–45 min
3:15 PM
Free time
Shower, nap, or float in the pool — let sore legs recover before the massage.
6:00 PM
Massage — Amihan Spa or Icthus Spa, Panagsama
Call or walk in earlier in the day to book your evening slot — both are small local spas along Panagsama and fill up fast at night.
Hotel → either spa: ~5 min walk ~₱400–600/pp, 1 hr
7:30 PM
Dinner — Tan-Aw
Asian fusion (Thai, Indonesian, Filipino) — a relaxed sit-down treat after canyoneering and a massage without breaking the budget. Mains run roughly ₱250–350.
Spa → Tan-Aw: ~5–10 min walk
03 Full chill day in Moalboal, evening transfer Moalboal → Cebu City Sat, Nov 21
Wake naturally
Sleep in, no alarm
No transfer today — you're staying an extra night in Moalboal, so there's nowhere to be.
8:30 AM
Breakfast
Included at Kuadros Hotel & Suites.
9:30 AM
Stroll & swim at White Beach (Basdaku)
A real soft-sand swimming beach, calm and shallow — a nice contrast to the coral-rubble shoreline back at the hotel. Take your time, no need to rush back.
Panagsama → White Beach: ~6 km, 12–15 min by tricycle/habal-habal Entrance ~₱10–20/pp + ₱25/pp Moalboal environmental fee · ride ~₱150–200 round trip, shared
12:30 PM
Lunch — Ven'z Kitchen
Affordable, no-frills Filipino comfort food back near Panagsama.
White Beach → Panagsama: ~6 km, 12–15 min back
Afternoon
Free time
Nap, swim off Panagsama again, browse the dive shops, or just sit by the water. Leave enough time to pack before the evening transfer.
5:00 PM
Dinner — Tan-Aw
Asian fusion, mains ~₱250–350 — early, so you're not eating on the road.
Hotel → Tan-Aw: ~5 min walk
5:45 PM
Check out of Kuadros Hotel & Suites
6:00 PM
Van transfer to Cebu City
Typically 2.5–3.5 hrs depending on traffic.
Moalboal → Cebu City: ~90 km by van
~9:00 PM
Arrive Cebu City, check in
Straight to bed after check-in — tomorrow's a full day.
04 City tour, jet ski & aqua dining Cebu City Sun, Nov 22
7:00 AM
Breakfast
Included at your Cebu City hotel. Motorbikes are already with you today (likely 2 units for the 4 of you, riding in pairs), so no separate pickup stop needed.
8:00 AM
Downtown heritage walk
Magellan's Cross and the Basilica del Santo Niño are right next to each other (a couple minutes apart), then it's a short walk to Fort San Pedro. Park the bikes once and walk the loop.
Magellan's Cross ↔ Basilica: ~1 min walk · Basilica → Fort San Pedro: ~10 min walk Fort San Pedro entry ~₱30/pp
12:00 PM
Lechon lunch — Zubuchon (N. Escario St)
Classic Cebu-style crispy skin — order by the kilo and split it as a group. Not walkable from the heritage sites, so ride over on the motorbikes. CNT Lechon nearer downtown is a cheaper alt if you'd rather skip the ride.
Fort San Pedro → Zubuchon: ~2.5 km, ~10 min by motorbike
1:30 PM
Souvenir shopping — Taboan Market
Dried mangoes, otap, and dried fish at local prices; a mall (Ayala or SM) nearby if you'd rather shop indoors.
Zubuchon → Taboan Market: ~2 km, ~10 min by motorbike
2:15 PM
Swing by the hotel to drop everything off
Leave the souvenirs (and anything else you don't want getting wet or sandy) at the hotel before heading out to the water — no need to lug it all to Mactan.
Taboan → hotel: ~2–3 km, ~10 min by motorbike
2:30 PM
Ride to Mactan (Maribago/Buyong)
Where Cebu's jet ski and water sports operators are actually based, since Moalboal is a protected reef area. Cross the Mactan–Mandaue bridges to get there.
Hotel → Maribago: ~17–18 km, ~30–45 min by motorbike
3:15 – 4:15 PM
Jet ski, Maribago/Buyong Beach
Book on the spot at one of the beachfront water-sports stalls, or reserve ahead online if you want a set time slot. Jet skis usually seat 1–2, so figure on 2 units for the 4 of you (or take turns on one if you'd rather split the cost).
~₱1,500–2,000 per jet ski, 15–30 min · ~₱3,000–4,000 total for 2 units
4:30 PM
Rinse off, freshen up
Quick break before crossing over to the CCLEX side.
5:00 PM
Ride across the CCLEX to South Road Properties (SRP)
The bridge itself is 8.9 km, Cebu's longest — a scenic ride in its own right.
Maribago → SRP: ~15–18 km, ~20–25 min by motorbike Toll ~₱51 (motorcycle, one way)
5:30 – 7:00 PM
CCLEX viewing deck at sunset
Right at the SRP end of the bridge — a popular sunset/photo stop, free to visit. Take your time here since it's the evening's main view stop.
7:15 PM
Ride back across the CCLEX to Maribago
Same bridge, in reverse — timed to land you at Aqua Bistro for your booking.
SRP → Maribago: ~15–18 km, ~20–25 min by motorbike Toll ~₱51 (motorcycle, one way)
8:15 PM
Aqua dining experience — Aqua Bistro, Maribago
Floating restaurant over the water in Mactan — the "aqua dining" stop for tonight. Worth booking/confirming a table ahead since it's popular at sunset hours.
Night
Ride back to your hotel
Maribago is on Mactan island — back across the Mactan–Mandaue bridges to the city.
Maribago → Cebu City hotel: ~17–18 km, ~30–40 min
If you want more
Sirao Flower Farm, Tops Lookout, Sky Ranch fun park
Dropping the paid skydeck frees up a little time, but the day is still a full one with jet ski, CCLEX, and Aqua Bistro. Let me know if you'd rather swap one of these in for something already on the list rather than stack on top of it.
